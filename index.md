# Kite Finance Privacy Policy

**Last updated: September 16, 2026**

Kite Finance is designed to help you manage your personal finances while keeping your financial information private.

## Data Collection

Kite Finance does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal or financial data.

The developer does not have access to the financial information you enter into Kite Finance.

## Local Data Storage

Your financial data, including records, categories, and other information you create within Kite Finance, is stored locally on your device.

Kite Finance does not automatically upload your financial data to external servers or cloud services.

## Analytics and Tracking

Kite Finance does not use analytics, advertising trackers, or other tracking technologies to collect information about your use of the application.

## Internet and Third-Party Services

Kite Finance does not transmit your financial data to third-party services.

## Data Control

Because your financial data is stored locally on your device, you are responsible for managing and protecting that data and any backups you create.

Uninstalling the application may affect locally stored application data. You should maintain appropriate backups of any data you wish to preserve.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if Kite Finance's functionality or data practices change. Any changes will be reflected on this page, along with an updated revision date.
